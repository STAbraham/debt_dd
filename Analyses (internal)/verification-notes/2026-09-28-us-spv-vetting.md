# US (Delaware LLC) SPV vetting (agent report, 2026-09-28)

1. US WHT SPV→Panama fund — CONFIRMED identical to parent loan: disregarded LLC's payments treated as owner's (Pub. 515); 30% §881(a) unless §881(c) portfolio interest. Either LLC (own EIN) or parent acts as withholding agent, collects W-8BEN-E, files 1042/1042-S. LLC can be named obligor; registered form needs only book-entry/re-registration transfer. Flags: 10% test applies to parent (warrants count via §318); FATCA ch. 4 applies to US-source interest — fund must certify ch. 4 status (Panama has Model 1 IGA) or 30% FATCA WHT regardless. Neither arises with SG borrower.
2. Spread taxation — CONFIRMED disregarded by default (Reg. §301.7701-3(b)(1)(ii)); income/expense on parent's 1120, nets against NOLs. Delaware: no income tax on LLC not doing business in DE; annual LLC tax **CORRECTED to USD 400** (HB 400, signed 21 May 2026, TY2026+, first due 1 Jun 2027; 6 Del. C. §18-1107(b)); no annual report.
3. PH–US treaty via disregarded LLC — PARTLY CONFIRMED / BIR PRACTICE UNVERIFIABLE. Treaty Art. 12(2) 15%; **no LOB article** (1976 treaty); Art. 3(1)(b) look-through only for partnerships/estates/trusts, DREs not addressed. IRS: Form 8802 line 4i for DRE; Form 6166 issued in owner's name (DREs "not considered U.S. residents within the meaning of the residence article"); fee $185. US–Mexico competent-authority agreement (2005) shows owner-level acceptance elsewhere; no PH equivalent. No BIR RMC/RMO/ITAD ruling found accepting a US DRE via owner's TRC → documentary mismatch risk under RMO 14-2021 (payee "LLC" vs TRC naming parent). Alternatives: (a) parent lends directly to ZFPI on the PH leg; (b) pre-clearance ITAD ruling; (c) LLC elects corporate status (fixes residence, loses NOL offset).
4. Bankruptcy remoteness — CONFIRMED. Standard features; limits: GGP (2009) independent managers owe duties to entity, may authorize filing; substantive consolidation (Owens Corning 3d Cir. 2005; Augie/Restivo 2d Cir. 1988) — defence is strict separateness; §362 stay if SPV files; 6 Del. C. §18-1101(b),(c); outright waivers of right to file unenforceable (Intervention Energy 2016; Lake Michigan Beach 2016) — consent must sit with a manager retaining duties. Non-con opinion USD 5–35K; lenders often forgo non-con/true-sale on ~USD 5M loan-on-loan (market practice, negotiable).
5. UCC perfection/enforcement — CONFIRMED. Membership interests: general intangible → UCC-1 in Delaware (parent as debtor, §9-307(e)); Art. 8 opt-in (§8-103(c)) + certificate → control, priority over filers. SPV's note = instrument (possession/filing); loan/security rights = general intangibles (filing; SPV located in DE). Deposit accounts by control (DACA, §9-104). Enforcement §9-610 public/private disposition, commercially reasonable; §9-612(b) 10-day notice safe harbor; §9-620 strict foreclosure (20-day objection). No court; 3–6 weeks realistic.
6. Cost/setup — CONFIRMED except annual tax. Formation fee $110; 1–3 business days (expedite $50–1,000); registered agent $50–300/yr; no residency requirement for managers; no audit. Independent manager providers (CSC, CT, Wilmington Trust, GSS, Vistra) don't publish rates — budget USD 3–10K/yr [market practice, not documentable].
7. Lender view — PARTLY CONFIRMED. Delaware LLC default SPV for US-lender warehouses; NY funds lend into LatAm via Delaware/Cayman SPVs (CoVenture→Digitt $50M). Asia deals more often Cayman/Singapore; no public Delaware SPV vs PH receivables example. Non-US fund may resist: interest becomes US-source (W-8/FATCA/portfolio-interest conditions); ECI — origination not covered by §864(b)(2) safe harbor; AM 2009-010 found ECI for "considerable, continuous and regular" US origination via US agent — single facility negotiated from Panama with no US office should not create USTB, but fund counsel will diligence and some funds have blanket no-US-borrower policies; Chapter 11 exposure / US counsel. OFAC/KYC neutral or favorable. PH security enforcement unaffected by SPV domicile.
8. BSP/PH side — CONFIRMED unchanged: Notice within 1 month, registration within 6 months of utilization if AAB FX to be used; docs not notarized; DST 0.75% payable by ZFPI.
