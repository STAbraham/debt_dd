# US tax vetting (agent report, 2026-09-28)

1. 30% default withholding on US-source interest to foreign corp — CONFIRMED. §881(a)(1), §1442(a). OID also covered.
2. No US–Panama income tax treaty; TIEA only (signed 30 Nov 2010, in force 18 Apr 2011) — CONFIRMED. IRS treaty A–Z list; State Dept TIAS 11-418.1.
3. Portfolio interest exemption — CONFIRMED with clarifications:
   - Registered form §881(c)(2)(B)(i); W-8BEN-E (or W-8IMY + partner W-8s for a partnership fund).
   - 10% shareholder test: 10% of total combined voting power; §318 attribution applies; **§318(a)(4): options/warrants count as owned stock**. For a partnership lender the test runs at partner level (Reg. §1.871-14(g)(3)(i)).
   - Contingent interest §871(h)(4)(A)(i): by reference to receipts/cash flow, income/profits, property value, dividends. Fixed/floating (SOFR) fine.
   - Bank exclusion §881(c)(3)(A) — non-bank credit fund generally outside it.
   - Related-CFC exclusion §881(c)(3)(C).
   - ECI is a separate check if the fund is treated as engaged in a US lending business.
4. §956 post-T.D. 9859 — CONFIRMED with conditions. Reg. §1.956-1(a)(2) reduces the tentative §956 amount by the hypothetical §245A DRD (domestic C-corp; §246(c) 365-day holding; non-hybrid). Pledge of ≥66⅔% CFC voting stock + negative covenants = indirect pledge of CFC assets (Reg. §1.956-2(c)). E&P cap: §956(a)/(b)(1) "applicable earnings" excludes deficits — zero if no positive accumulated or current-year E&P; a profitable year creates exposure.
5. §163(j) small-business exemption — CORRECTED figure: §448(c) gross receipts test = **$31M for tax years beginning 2025 (Rev. Proc. 2024-40), $32M for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32)**; aggregated across controlled group (PH sub counts); C-corp is not a "syndicate" so tax-shelter carve-out doesn't apply.
6. Singapore Pte. Ltd. eligible for check-the-box — CONFIRMED. Only "Public Limited Company" is per se (Reg. §301.7701-2(b)(8)(i)). Default for single-owner foreign LLC entity is corporation, so affirmative Form 8832 election needed; effective up to 75 days retroactive.
7. Gross-up clauses standard (LSTA MCAPs "Taxes" section; Excluded Taxes carve-outs incl. failure to deliver W-8) — CONFIRMED.
8. FTC: zero §904(a) limitation when no US tax; 10-yr carryforward / 1-yr carryback per basket (§904(c)); alternative §164 deduction (§275(a)(4)) — CONFIRMED.
