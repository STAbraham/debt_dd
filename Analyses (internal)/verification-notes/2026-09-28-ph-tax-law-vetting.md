# Philippine tax/law vetting (agent report, 2026-09-28)

1. 20% FWT on foreign-loan interest to NRFC — CONFIRMED. NIRC §28(B)(5)(a); untouched by CMEPA (RA 12214, 2025); BIR RMC 19-2024 restates. (General NRFC rate is 25%; foreign-loan interest specifically 20%.)
2. Treaties — CORRECTED article: PH–US interest is **Art. 12** (15% general; 10% public bond issues; exempt for government/Ex-Im/OPIC). PH–Singapore Art. 11: 15%; 10% only public bonds; Art. 11(6) denies treaty rate on related-party excess interest; beneficial-owner/conduit scrutiny. No PH–Panama treaty (BIR list of 43 DTAs) → 20%.
3. Treaty relief (RMO 14-2021 / RMC 77-2021) — CONFIRMED. Withholding agent may apply treaty rate at source if payee provides Tax Residency Certificate + BIR Form 0901 before payment; agent files Request for Confirmation with ITAD by last day of 4th month after year-end. Otherwise 20% withheld and payee files TTRA for refund of differential.
4. DST — CORRECTED wording: CMEPA (eff. 1 Jul 2025; RR 19-2025) rewrote §179 as "75% of 1% of issue price" (was P1.50/P200) — same 0.75%. Applies to loans "issued abroad wherein the object of contract is located or used in the Philippines" — offshore execution doesn't avoid it if proceeds used in PH. One DST covers loan + security docs. Intercompany advances are subject (CIR v. Filinvest, G.R. 163653, 2011). USD 5M ≈ PHP 2.1M ≈ USD 37.5k per PH leg; fund→US parent leg outside PH DST.
5. CIT — CONFIRMED 25%; 20% if net taxable income ≤ P5M and total assets ≤ P100M. CREATE MORE (RA 12066, Nov 2024) didn't change these or NRFC interest FWT. Interest arbitrage rule §34(B)(1) (deduction cut by 20% of interest income subjected to final tax). Deficit company: deductibility feeds NOLCO (3-yr carryover), not current cash tax.
6. BSP foreign loans (FX Manual, May 2025; Circulars 1030/1124/1171) — CONFIRMED with mechanics: prior approval only for public-sector or publicly-guaranteed loans. Private non-guaranteed loans: registration needed only if to be serviced with FX bought from AABs. Notice to BSP (Annex E.3) + signed agreement within 1 month of signing; registration application within 1 month of drawdown (short-term) / 6 months of utilization (MLT). Loan docs must not be notarized. All foreign loans reported to BSP-IOD regardless. BSP FAQ (Mar 2026): advances from foreign parents/shareholders/affiliates ARE foreign loans → intercompany loans registrable. Borrowers with own FX may skip registration but still Notice+report. Late-filing fees abolished (Circ. 1171, 2023). Non-QB credit card issuer is NOT in the bank/QB exemption.
7. PPSA (RA 11057) — CONFIRMED. Perfection by registration/possession/control (§12); outright transfers of receivables perfected the same way. **PPSR live since 3 Feb 2025** (LRA Circular 02-2025; ppsr.lra.gov.ph). Enforcement: §47 self-help repossession if agreed and no breach of peace; §48 direct collection of receivables; §49–50 commercially reasonable disposition. Notice to account debtors needed to redirect payments.
8. Securitization Act (RA 9267) — CONFIRMED + nuance: SEC-approved plan; SPE as corporation registered with SEC; SPE as trust administered by licensed trust entity (no SEC registration). **Prior BSP endorsement required where originator is a BSP-supervised financial intermediary** — ZFPI qualifies.
9. FRIA (RA 10142) — CONFIRMED. §16 Commencement/Stay Order suspends enforcement incl. security; §60 lien not impaired but enforcement may be suspended; court may allow foreclosure if property not necessary for rehabilitation.
10. RCC (RA 11232) — CONFIRMED. §42 dividends only from unrestricted retained earnings; §37 capital decrease needs 2/3 shareholders + SEC approval (withheld if prejudicial to creditors).
11. Upstream guarantees / BSP regime — CONFIRMED (guarantees, rate cap); UNVERIFIED (encumbrance limits). Guarantees permitted if authorized in AOI and for corporate benefit; ultra vires risk (RCC §§35/44) if subsidiary guarantees parent debt with no benefit — document benefit (proceeds on-lent to ZFPI). No statutory financial-assistance prohibition. RA 10870 §4: BSP supervises all card issuers; non-bank issuers need BSP authority (Circ. 1003, MORNBFI). Rate cap: **Circular 1165 (Feb 2023): 3%/mo, 36% p.a.**; installment add-on max 1%/mo; cash advance fee max P200 (1098 was 2%/24%). No specific prohibition found on a non-bank issuer encumbering/selling receivables or guaranteeing — check MORNBFI C-Regs and ZFPI's authority conditions (likely notification).
12. Transfer pricing — CONFIRMED. RR 2-2013 arm's-length covers intercompany financing. BIR Form 1709 required for (among others) taxpayers with net operating losses in current + two preceding years — loss-making ZFPI likely in scope. Formal TP documentation only if gross revenue > P150M AND RPTs > P90M, or per-transaction > P15M (interest).
13. VAT / doing business — UNVERIFIED (best authority): sale of receivables not itself a "sale of services"; buyer's discount/interest is the taxable item — GRT (§122, 5%/1%) if buyer is BSP-supervised NBFI, else 12% VAT (§108). No BIR ruling on card-receivable sales found → get ruling/Big-4 opinion. FIA (RA 7042) §3(d): isolated transactions ≠ doing business; single loan/purchase by foreign SPV defensible; revolving purchase program with PH servicer riskier → structure offshore with ZFPI as servicer. RA 9474/8556 not triggered by offshore lender with no PH presence.
